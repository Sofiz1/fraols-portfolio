01

The Genesis

Every legacy begins with ignition. My journey started in the ranks of the ELPA Cycling Club, forging raw potential into tactical precision on the unforgiving asphalt of Ethiopia. Now riding for the elite Addis Ababa Police Force Cycling Club, the mission has evolved. It's no longer just about pushing the boundaries of speed—it's about commanding the peloton, mastering aerodynamics, and representing the absolute pinnacle of Ethiopian cycling.

02

The Philosophy of Pain

Cycling isn't just about speed it's a conversation with suffering. Every pedal stroke is a negotiation between the mind's desire to quit and the body's refusal to surrender. Out here on the asphalt of Addis Ababa, the rhythm of the gears becomes a meditation. I don't just ride to win I ride to discover the absolute limits of human endurance.
